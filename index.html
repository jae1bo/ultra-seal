Our Services

Specialists in regrouting and sealing. We restore and protect every kind of tiled surface, indoors and out.

01

Regrouting

Old, cracked, or discoloured grout removed and replaced with fresh grout in your choice of colour. Tiles look brand new.

02

Tile & Grout Sealing

Premium sealant applied to lock out moisture, mould, and stains, keeping surfaces cleaner and better protected for years.

Shower Restoration

Waterproofing

Proper waterproofing for wet areas to prevent water damage, leaks, and structural issues beneath your tiles.
